Who we are

A Maldivian boatyard built on engineering discipline.

MGH WaveCraft is the marine manufacturing arm of MGH Group. From our boatyard in Thimarafushi we design, laminate, fit out and sea-trial fiberglass vessels for resort transfers, inter-island passenger ferries, excursion operations, supply logistics and specialised government use.

Every hull leaves our yard with documented lamination schedules, weighed resin ratios and a full sea-trial report — the same discipline you would expect from a European yard, delivered with Maldivian craftsmanship and local ocean knowledge.

The Fleet

Three series. One standard of build.

Each series shares the same hull engineering philosophy — deep-V forward sections, vinylester skin coats and hand-finished gelcoat — tuned for a different job on the water.

Wave Series

Luxury Transfers

Fast, quiet resort transfer boats with air-conditioned cabins and premium interiors for 12 to 40 guests.

Atlas Series

Passenger Ferries

Commercial inter-island ferries engineered for daily schedules, high passenger counts and low running costs.

Voyager Series

Supply & Utility

Working vessels for cargo, dive support, construction logistics and long-range excursion charter.

The boatyard

Thimarafushi. Where the hulls are born.

Our covered production halls hold multiple hull moulds in parallel, with dedicated bays for lamination, joinery, engine installation, electrical fit-out and finishing. Climate-controlled storage keeps resin and glass within specification year round.

Inside the facility
Craftsmanship

Measured, laminated, finished by hand.

Hand Lamination

Controlled resin-to-glass ratios, vinylester skin coats and full structural stringers bonded into the hull.

Marine Engineering

Weight distribution, propulsion matching and sea-keeping modelled before the first mould is opened.

Finishing

Wet-sanded and polished gelcoat, marine-grade joinery and stainless fittings installed to survey standard.
