Newsroom

News from the yard.

Launches, facility updates and engineering notes from MGH WaveCraft.

Launches

W-42 handed over in Thaa Atoll

The latest Wave Series transfer boat completed open-water trials and entered resort service this month, carrying 24 guests at a 28-knot cruise.

Facility

Second lamination hall opens at Thimarafushi

Additional covered capacity allows three large hulls to be laid up in parallel, cutting typical lead times by around six weeks.

Projects

65-seat excursion catamaran enters fit-out

A new wide-beam excursion platform for southern atoll operators has moved from lamination into joinery and systems installation.

Industry

Atlas ferries complete third monsoon season

Operators report schedule reliability above 98% across the southwest monsoon, with fuel burn holding under the original projection.

Engineering

Outboard rigging programme standardised

A single rigging and monitoring standard across all outboard-powered hulls simplifies crew training and spares across mixed fleets.

Refits

Twenty-year-old ferry hull returns to service

Structural rebuild, new engine beds and full gelcoat restoration return a veteran hull to daily commercial operation.
