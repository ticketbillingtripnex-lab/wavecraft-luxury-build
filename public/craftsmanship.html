Craftsmanship

A hull is only as good as the day it was laminated.

How WaveCraft turns resin, glass and engineering drawings into a vessel that survives twenty seasons in the Indian Ocean.

Fiberglass construction

Built in layers, checked at every one.

Each hull starts with a polished mould and an isophthalic gelcoat, followed by a vinylester skin coat that resists osmosis in warm tropical water. Chopped strand and biaxial glass are then laid up to a written schedule, with resin weighed rather than estimated.

Longitudinal stringers and transverse frames are bonded in and over-laminated, spreading engine and slamming loads across the hull instead of concentrating them at the transom.

Engineering

Hull lines, weight distribution and propeller matching are modelled before tooling. We size engines for the operator's real load, not the brochure figure.

Materials

Marine-grade resins, E-glass reinforcement, 316 stainless fittings and closed-cell foam coring where weight matters more than cost.

Marine quality

Hardness tests on cured laminate, dry-fit checks on every system and a documented file that follows the vessel from mould to handover.

Production process

Mould preparation, lamination, hull turn-out, structural bonding, deck joining, fit-out, painting, commissioning, sea trial, delivery.

The standard

Nine stages. No shortcuts between them.

Stage 01

Design & engineering

Route, payload and sea conditions translated into hull lines and a laminate schedule.

Stage 02

Mould preparation

Moulds polished, waxed and released; gelcoat thickness measured wet.

Stage 03

Lamination

Skin coat and structural laminate applied and rolled out layer by layer.

Stage 04

Structure

Stringers, bulkheads and engine beds bonded and over-laminated.

Stage 05

Deck join

Hull and deck bonded and mechanically fastened along the sheer.

Stage 06

Systems

Fuel, steering, electrical and plumbing installed, pressure-tested and labelled.

Stage 07

Fit-out

Joinery, upholstery, glazing and hardware installed by in-house trades.

Stage 08

Finishing

Wet sanding, compounding and polishing to a mirror gelcoat finish.

Stage 09

Sea trial & handover

Open-water trials, crew familiarisation and a complete documentation pack.

MGH WaveCraft designs and builds high-performance fiberglass vessels at our boatyard in Thimarafushi, Thaa Atoll — serving resorts, operators and government fleets across the Maldives and the wider Indian Ocean.
