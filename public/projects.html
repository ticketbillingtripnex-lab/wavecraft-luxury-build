Case study

Inter-island ferry route, Thaa Atoll.

The brief. A regional operator needed to carry 65 passengers between six islands, six days a week, on a fixed fuel budget — in a hull that could take a beam sea on the return leg.

Our approach. We adapted the Atlas hull with a finer entry, moved the fuel tanks aft to trim the running attitude, and specified twin 440 hp diesels rather than larger engines running hard.

The result. Scheduled crossings hold 22 knots at 78% load, fuel burn came in 14% under the operator's ceiling, and the vessel has kept its timetable through three monsoon seasons.

Before & after

Refit programme.

After — rebuilt structure, new engine beds, fresh gelcoat and a re-certified hull back in commercial operation.
