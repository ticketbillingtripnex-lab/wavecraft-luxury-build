Careers

Work where the boats are actually built.

WaveCraft employs laminators, joiners, marine electricians, engineers and coordinators at Thimarafushi and in Male'.

Training

Structured apprenticeships in lamination, joinery and marine electrics.

Accommodation

Staff housing, meals and scheduled transport for island-based roles.

Health cover

Medical insurance for team members and immediate family.

Progression

Skilled trades move into supervisor and QA roles as the yard grows.

Culture

Small teams, finished boats, visible results.

Every hull carries the names of the people who built it. Our teams own a vessel from lamination through to the sea trial, which is why quality problems get solved on the shop floor rather than in a report.
