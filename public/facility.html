Facility

The Thimarafushi boatyard.

Covered production halls, dedicated trade bays and direct lagoon access for launching and sea trials.

Manufacturing

Two covered halls hold multiple hull and deck moulds in parallel, with overhead lifting for hull turn-out.

Fiberglass process

Gelcoat, vinylester skin coat, chopped strand and biaxial laminate applied to a written schedule for every hull.

Engine installation

Aligned engine beds, sealed transoms, fuel systems pressure-tested and commissioned by our own technicians.

Workshop & joinery

Marine plywood, laminate and solid timber fit-out produced in-house, so interiors match the hull schedule instead of waiting on imports.

Sea trials

Every vessel completes speed runs, turning trials, bilge and steering checks in open water before we hand over the keys.

Maintenance & support

Scheduled servicing, hull repair, gelcoat restoration and emergency response for vessels operating anywhere in the atolls.

MGH WaveCraft designs and builds high-performance fiberglass vessels at our boatyard in Thimarafushi, Thaa Atoll — serving resorts, operators and government fleets across the Maldives and the wider Indian Ocean.
