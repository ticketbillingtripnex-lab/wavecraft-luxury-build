About

Eighteen years of Maldivian boatbuilding.

MGH WaveCraft was founded to prove a simple idea: a vessel built in the Maldives, by Maldivian craftsmen, for Maldivian waters, can outperform anything imported into them.

Company story

From a single mould to a full production yard.

WaveCraft began with one hull mould, a rented shed and a handful of laminators who had grown up around dhonis. The first boats were simple island transfer craft — but they were measured, documented and repeatable, and operators noticed.

Today the yard at Thimarafushi runs covered lamination halls, dedicated joinery and engine bays, and a sea-trial programme that every vessel must pass before handover. We build for resorts, ferry operators, dive centres, construction logistics companies and government agencies across all 26 atolls.

MGH Group

Part of a Maldivian group with deep roots.

WaveCraft is the marine manufacturing division of MGH Group, whose interests span logistics, construction supply, engineering services and island development. Group backing gives the yard something rare in small-scale boatbuilding: stable capital, procurement scale and an after-sales network that reaches every atoll we deliver to.

Vision

To make the Maldives the recognised centre of fiberglass boatbuilding in the Indian Ocean.

Mission

To engineer, build and support vessels that run reliably, day after day, in the conditions our clients actually face.

Promise

Documented builds, honest schedules and a yard that answers the phone long after handover.
